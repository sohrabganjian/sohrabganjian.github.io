    Quantum Resources Inspiration

Sohrab Ganjian

NRCan. Quantum Scientist.

Ottawa, Ontario

I am a quantum research scientist at Natural Resources Canada, working on quantum computing for geospatial applications. My research explores the feasibility and utility of quantum machine learning and optimization for problems in Earth observation and flood prediction. I investigate the capabilities of noisy intermediate-scale quantum (NISQ) devices and the potential for quantum approaches to compete with or improve upon leading classical approaches as quantum hardware advances.

I hold an MSc in Mathematics from the University of Ottawa, where I studied quantum cryptography under Dr. Anne Broadbent, focusing on the simulation of quantum homomorphic encryption. Prior to this, I earned a BSc with Distinction in Mathematics and a minor in Philosophy at Simon Fraser University, where I completed two NSERC-funded undergraduate research terms and a data analyst co-op placement at a Vancouver-based technology company. Working across academia, industry, and government has helped me adapt to different research and practical settings and approach a variety of problems.

I enjoy working on problems that bridge theory and practical applications, motivated by the potential for science to benefit humanity. My approach is grounded in persistence, resilience, and a willingness to work through difficult ideas.

I support open-source software and open scientific exchange, and I value making scientific ideas accessible through tutorials and community events, such as my talk at the 2026 uOttawa Qiskit Fall Fest.

I welcome conversations about quantum science and potential collaborations. Feel free to reach me through LinkedIn.

Please drop me a quick message on LinkedIn, and I’ll be happy to connect!
